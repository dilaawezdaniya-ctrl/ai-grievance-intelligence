# Problem Discovery

## 1. Product

**AI Grievance Intelligence & Officer Copilot**

## 2. Existing Ecosystem

MP CM Helpline 181 is an established grievance and citizen-service channel.

Citizens can register complaints through the helpline, portal and mobile applications and can check complaint status.

The system routes complaints to relevant departmental officers for resolution.

## 3. Existing Capabilities

* Complaint registration
* Complaint status tracking
* Department/category information
* Citizen portal
* Citizen mobile applications
* Officer applications
* WhatsApp chatbot
* Citizen feedback
* Multi-level grievance workflow

## 4. Evidence

Official CM Helpline sources were reviewed to understand the current grievance ecosystem.

Sources:

* CM Helpline About page
* Online Complaint form
* Grievance Status
* Public complaint records
* CM Helpline archival policy
* CM Helpline WhatsApp information

## 5. What We Know

The existing system already supports complaint registration, routing, status tracking, officer action and citizen feedback.

## 6. What We Do NOT Yet Know

We do not yet have sufficient evidence to claim that:

* complaints are frequently misrouted
* duplicate complaints are a widespread problem
* officers are overwhelmed by complaint volume
* incomplete complaints are a major problem
* existing routing is inefficient
* officers lack adequate information

These are research questions, not established facts.

## 7. Initial Opportunity Hypothesis

Large volumes of grievance information may create an opportunity for AI-assisted understanding, similarity detection, clustering, summarization and emerging-issue monitoring.

## 8. Product Direction

Rather than replacing CM Helpline, we propose an AI intelligence layer that can assist existing grievance workflows.

## 9. Initial AI Capabilities

1. Complaint understanding
2. Classification assistance
3. Entity extraction
4. Similar complaint detection
5. Complaint clustering
6. Emerging issue detection
7. Officer copilot
8. Citizen-friendly explanations

## 10. Human-in-the-Loop Principle

AI recommendations should assist human officials.

AI should not independently make final government decisions.
